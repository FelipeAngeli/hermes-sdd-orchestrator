# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and focus criticism on ideas and code, never on people. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/FelipeAngeli). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation of anyone who violates this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
